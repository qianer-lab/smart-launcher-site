Privacy Policy

Effective: September 3, 2026

Smart Launcher is a macOS application designed around a local-first architecture. This policy explains what information the application may process and how optional paid licensing may affect data handling.

1. Core launcher data

Smart Launcher may read information needed to provide launcher functionality, such as installed application metadata, localized application names, application icons and launcher layout information available on the Mac. Core launcher functionality is designed to operate locally on the device.

2. Local Learning and Smart recommendations

If Local Learning is enabled, Smart Launcher may process local application-activity signals to improve recommendations. The product is designed so that its core Smart Engine does not require a cloud AI backend. Local Learning is an explicit, user-controlled capability.

3. Optional contextual signals

Features such as Calendar Context may require permission to access relevant macOS data. Access is optional and subject to macOS permission controls. Privacy and permission surfaces are kept separate from purchase messaging.

4. Payments and Pro licensing

When paid checkout is enabled, payments are expected to be processed by Dodo Payments as Merchant of Record. Payment information is handled by the payment provider rather than by Smart Launcher directly. Pro activation may require limited licensing information, such as license status, activation state, validation timestamps and a device-related identifier necessary to enforce license limits.

5. Analytics and advertising

The current pre-release website does not include third-party advertising or behavioral analytics. If privacy-affecting services are added later, this policy will be updated before they are used in production.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local Smart Launcher data remains subject to the application's local storage and reset controls. Payment and transaction records processed by Dodo Payments are retained under Dodo Payments' applicable legal and compliance requirements.

7. Security

Smart Launcher is designed to keep sensitive local behavior data on the Mac where practical. License credentials and activation state are intended to use appropriate macOS secure-storage mechanisms.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the product moves from pre-release to paid release. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

9. Contact

Smart Launcher is an independent software product. The production support email will be published before paid checkout is enabled. During pre-release, developer information is available through the qianer-lab GitHub organization.

Terms of Use

Effective: September 3, 2026

Smart Launcher is currently pre-release software. These terms establish the intended product and license model but do not activate paid checkout by themselves.

1. Product

Smart Launcher is a native macOS application launcher supporting macOS 15 and later, including compatible Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Features may vary by operating-system version and product edition.

2. Free edition

The Free edition is intended to include the core launcher experience, Core Smart recommendations, Local Learning, basic Calendar Context and a starter Smart Rules allowance. Free functionality remains usable when Pro activation is skipped or unavailable.

3. Pro edition

The Pro edition is intended to add deeper and reusable intelligence features, including Advanced Context, advanced Calendar Intelligence, expanded Smart Rules and Automation for user-approved rules. Exact commercial terms and price will be displayed before a customer is charged.

4. License activation

A Pro license may be subject to activation and device limits. Activation can have states including active, invalid, device-limit reached, temporarily offline or revoked. A failed or revoked Pro activation does not remove the user's Free-edition rights.

5. Payments

When production checkout is enabled, eligible paid transactions are expected to be processed by Dodo Payments as Merchant of Record. The checkout page or purchase flow will display the amount, currency, applicable taxes and relevant buyer terms before payment.

6. Acceptable use

7. Availability and changes

Pre-release features may change before general availability. After release, Smart Launcher may modify or discontinue features when necessary for compatibility, security, legal compliance or product maintenance.

8. Warranty limitation

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the software is provided without guarantees that it will be uninterrupted or error-free. Nothing in these terms limits consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

9. Contact

The production support email and final commercial contact details will be added before paid checkout is enabled.

Refund Policy

Effective: September 3, 2026

This policy applies once Smart Launcher Pro paid checkout is enabled.

1. Refund request window

Refund requests should be submitted within 30 days of the successful transaction. Dodo Payments' transaction system supports eligible full or partial refunds initiated within 30 days of the transaction date.

2. Common eligible cases

  • Duplicate purchases or duplicate charges.
  • A confirmed technical problem that prevents the purchased Pro license from being activated or used on a supported Mac and cannot be reasonably resolved.
  • Other cases where a refund is required by applicable consumer law.

3. License handling after refund

If a Pro purchase is refunded, the associated Pro entitlement may be revoked. Smart Launcher will continue to operate with the functionality available in the Free edition.

4. Refund processing

Approved refunds are processed through the original payment channel. Processing time after approval may depend on the payment method and financial institution.

5. How to request a refund

The production support email and purchase-support instructions will be published before paid checkout is enabled. Customers will also receive payment records through the Dodo Payments transaction flow.

6. Statutory rights

This policy does not limit any refund, cancellation or consumer-protection rights that apply under mandatory local law.
