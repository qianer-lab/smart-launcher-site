Product Features

A familiar way to launch,
with a smarter next step.

Smart Launcher is a native macOS launcher. Classic preserves the app grid and folder experience, while Smart progressively surfaces useful apps from patterns learned on your Mac.

Classic Launcher

Keep the app grid, folders, pages and direct launching workflow. Smart enhances the launcher without replacing its core behavior.

Smart Page

Continue, Recent, New & Changed and related sections surface apps that are more likely to matter now.

Local Learning

Usage patterns are formed on the Mac. The core Smart Engine does not require a cloud AI service or uploading a complete app-usage history.

Usage Insights

Inspect local timing and usage-pattern signals used by Smart instead of treating recommendations as an opaque black box.

Calendar Context

With explicit permission, calendar context can become an additional Smart signal. Free and Pro provide different levels of contextual capability.

Smart Rules

Turn repeatable context and usage patterns into reusable rules. Free includes starter capability; Pro expands rules and user-approved automation.

Menu Bar

Use a native macOS menu bar entry for quick access to Smart Launcher and related settings.

Universal Mac

A Universal application for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, supporting macOS 15 and later.

English + Chinese

The product UI supports English and Simplified Chinese while installed apps follow their own localized display names.

Product status

Public documentation distinguishes shipping from planned features.

Capabilities available in a public release are described as available. Pro extensions that have not reached the public release are marked Planned / Coming soon rather than advertised as already delivered.
